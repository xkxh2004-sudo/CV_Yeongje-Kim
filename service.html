Service

Approach to Service

My engagement connects my interests in learning and technology with practical support for educational institutions and community members. Through smartphone education and a learning club at the Seoul Metropolitan Senior Welfare Center, I have supported older adults with guided practice and troubleshooting. These activities reflect my commitment to making technology learning accessible and responsive to learners’ needs. I have also contributed to institutional outreach by developing and launching the Learning Sciences program’s official website at Korea University. Across these activities, I aim to apply my educational expertise to support access to information and opportunities for participation in learning.

Service

Assistant Instructor, Smartphone Learning Club for Older Adults

Seoul Metropolitan Senior Welfare Center · Nov. 2024 - Jan. 2026

Facilitated weekly learning club sessions for older adults learning to use smartphones for everyday problem solving. This experience shaped my understanding of digital literacy as a social and emotional process, not only a technical skill. Working closely with older learners showed how confidence, peer support, accessible explanations, and authentic daily tasks can change the way learners approach unfamiliar technologies.

Assistant Instructor, Smartphone Education Program for Older Adults

Seoul Metropolitan Senior Welfare Center · Jul. 2024 - Dec. 2024

Provided step-by-step guidance and troubleshooting support in a smartphone education program for older adults. This experience helped me see how small instructional choices, pacing, and learner-centered support can reduce anxiety and make technology learning more sustainable.
