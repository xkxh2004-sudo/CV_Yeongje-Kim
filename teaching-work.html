Teaching & Work

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach centers on helping learners make sense of ideas through active participation, meaningful tasks, and support responsive to their needs. I draw on learner-centered and embodied approaches, connecting concepts to activities that learners can experience and discuss. In a fifth-grade math camp, I designed and implemented activities grounded in embodied cognition. My experience providing individualized academic support and step-by-step technology guidance also informs my emphasis on scaffolding. Across these settings, my goal is to create learning experiences that recognize differences in prior knowledge and support learners as they develop understanding and confidence.

Through this work, I gained experience connecting theory-driven design, classroom implementation, and qualitative and quantitative data collection.
